# User Story: Scientific Head of Collection

## 1. Persona

**Name:** Wanja

**Role:** Head of Collection, Entomological Collection

**Description:**
Wanja oversees the scientific quality, strategic development, and long-term management of an entomological collection. He is responsible for ensuring the collection’s scientific relevance, documenting its strengths, and supporting research activities. His work focuses on understanding the significance, development, and knowledge value of type specimen holdings across the collection.

---

## 2. Scenario and Motivation

Wanja requires a comprehensive understanding of the collection’s type specimens and their scientific importance. Currently, information about type specimens, catalogues, taxonomic coverage, specialists, and historical interpretations may be distributed across collection databases, publications, archival records, and specimen documentation, making strategic assessment difficult and time-consuming.

A **Type Specimen Knowledge Base** would provide centralized, structured access to information about type specimens, catalogues, taxonomic groups, geographic coverage, historical documentation, and collection development. Advanced search and analytical capabilities would enable Wanja to evaluate the scientific strengths of the collection, identify knowledge gaps, and support long-term collection planning.

Using the Knowledge Base, Wanja could:

* Obtain a complete overview of documented type specimens in the collection.
* Assess the taxonomic and geographic coverage of type holdings.
* Identify the strongest and most comprehensively represented groups.
* Explore the historical development of the collection and its scientific interpretation.
* Review published catalogues and their contributions to collection knowledge.
* Identify specialists who have documented and studied the collection.
* Support strategic decisions regarding collection development, digitization, and research priorities.

---

## 3. Competency Questions (CQs)

### CQ1. Type Specimen Inventory

Which type specimens are documented in the collection?

### CQ2. Type Specimen Quantification

How many type specimens exist in the collection?

### CQ3. Taxonomic Distribution

How are type specimens distributed across taxonomic groups?

### CQ4. Collection Strengths by Taxon

Which taxonomic groups are best represented in the collection?

### CQ5. Geographic Coverage

What geographic regions are represented by the collection’s type specimens?

### CQ6. Published Catalogues

Which catalogues documenting the collection have been published?

### CQ7. Specialist Contributions

Which specialists have documented or studied the collection’s type specimens?

### CQ8. Historical Interpretation

How has the scientific interpretation of the collection changed over time?

### CQ9. Collection Development

How has the collection developed throughout its history?

### CQ10. Collection Strength Assessment

What are the principal scientific strengths of the collection?

### CQ11. Knowledge Contribution of Catalogues

Which catalogues contribute the most knowledge and documentation about the collection?
