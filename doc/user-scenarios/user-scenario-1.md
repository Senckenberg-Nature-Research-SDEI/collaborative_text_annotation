# User Story: Butterfly Researcher

## 1. Persona

**Name:** Alex  
**Role:** Butterfly Researcher

### Description
Alex studies the biology, systematics, and ecology of butterflies and curates a collection of Lepidoptera specimens. His research focuses on the monitoring, taxonomy, and conservation of European butterflies, particularly those from the Macaronesian Islands. He frequently works with type specimens to verify species identities, investigate taxonomic relationships, and analyze biodiversity patterns. :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}

## 2. Scenario and Motivation

Alex regularly consults type specimens and their associated metadata to support taxonomic, ecological, and conservation research. Currently, much of this information is scattered across specimen labels, publications, collection databases, and PDF catalogs, making searches time-consuming and inefficient. :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}

A Type Specimen Knowledge Base would provide centralized, structured access to information on type specimens, including:

- Taxonomic names
- Synonymies
- Collection locations
- Repositories
- Images
- Genetic resources

Advanced search and query capabilities would enable Alex to quickly retrieve relevant information, compare taxa, and explore temporal and geographic patterns in species descriptions. :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}

### Using the Knowledge Base, Alex could:

- Verify whether a newly recognized species has previously been described under another name.
- Identify the institution or collection where a type specimen is deposited.
- Access linked resources such as photographs, DNA barcodes, and related publications.
- Analyze trends in species discovery and description over time.
- Explore the geographic distribution and provenance of type specimens. :contentReference[oaicite:3]{index=3}
